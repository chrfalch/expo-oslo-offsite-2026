# Oslo Offsite support

Oslo Offsite is the participant guide for the September 2026 offsite in Oslo. It includes the schedule, travel and accommodation details, places to explore, and a personal packing checklist.

For help, incorrect guide information, or beta feedback, contact **christian@expo.dev**. Include your device model, app version, the screen involved, and what happened. Send only the details needed to explain the issue.

Choose your attendee profile the first time you open the app. You can change it later from the profile screen. Your saved places and packing checklist stay on your device.

The bundled guide works offline. Maps, external websites, and app updates need a connection. Android opens destinations in an external Maps app or website.

Read the [Oslo Offsite privacy policy](https://oslo-offsite-2026.expo.app/privacy.html) for information about app updates, diagnostics, and your saved choices.
