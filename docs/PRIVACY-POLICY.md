# Oslo Offsite privacy policy

Oslo Offsite is provided by Christian Magnus Falch for participants in the Oslo offsite. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## The guide and your choices

The app contains a guide with the offsite schedule, attendee names, travel details, accommodation, and places to visit. This information is included in the app so the guide can work offline. Anyone with access to the app can view its bundled content. Choosing an attendee personalizes the guide; it is not a sign-in or identity check.

Your selected attendee, saved places, and packing checklist are stored on your device. The app does not provide an account service or synchronize these choices to other devices. Changing the selected attendee keeps each attendee's saved choices separate on that device.

## App updates and diagnostics

The app uses Expo services to download compatible app updates and to help us understand reliability and performance. Network requests to these services include information needed to deliver the update or process the request, such as app and runtime versions and the requesting device's IP address.

Expo Observe collects diagnostic information, including a randomly generated installation identifier, session identifiers, app and update versions, device model, operating-system version, language, screen-navigation and startup timings, and error or crash information. Depending on the platform and event, diagnostics can also include memory, battery, thermal and network state and summaries of network-request performance.

We use this information to investigate failures and improve the app. The app does not deliberately attach your selected attendee name, packing choices, or saved places to diagnostic events. Dynamic place and location identifiers are filtered from navigation telemetry.

Diagnostics are sent to Expo, our app infrastructure provider. Events recorded offline may be stored on the device and sent later when a connection is available. The app contains no advertising, advertising identifiers, in-app purchases, or payment processing.

Expo may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Its privacy policy describes its international-transfer arrangements and the ways to contact it about information it handles.

## Weather

The app requests a shared Oslo forecast from our weather endpoint on Expo hosting. Expo receives the network information needed to serve that request, including your IP address. Our endpoint fetches and caches public forecast data from MET Norway using fixed Oslo coordinates. It does not forward your IP address, selected attendee, or device location to MET Norway. A copy of the forecast is stored on your device for offline use.

## Maps and external websites

iOS displays maps using Apple Maps. The current Android configuration opens destinations in an external Maps app or website. Opening a destination or website shares the requested destination or page with that service, which handles requests under its own privacy policy. The app does not request your device's current location or access your photo library.

## Retention and requests

Your saved choices remain on the device until the app's data is removed or the choices are changed. Device backup and restore settings can affect whether local app data returns after reinstallation.

Expo's documentation states that metric data is retained for a minimum of 60 days. Removing the app or its local data does not remove diagnostics already sent to Expo. We do not promise an automatic deletion date for those records.

For questions about the app's handling of information, corrections to guide information, or a request concerning your data, contact **christian@expo.dev**. We will assess the request and explain the available steps, including any limits on identifying a diagnostic record associated only with a random installation identifier.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The published policy will show its latest revision date.
